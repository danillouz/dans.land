Dan's Land

Greetings traveler. I am Dan, the guardian of this domain.

In ages past I soared through the heavens astride great metal dragons. But in the year 2012, I answered the call of a different magic: the ancient art of software crafting.

From my Fortress of Contemplation I now serve the Legendary Builders of Framer. As Battlemage of the Backend Voids, I wield arcane spells and command vast armies of immortal Gophers to SHAPE THE DIGITAL AETHER TO MY WILL!

But that is not all. I also practice the Dark Arts of Pastromancy. Fueled by dough, cinnamon buns make my power (and waistline) grow!

When I'm not keeping the digital aether online (or resisting the baked temptations of evil), you will find me lost in tomes of epic adventure (mostly fantasy), or exploring the countryside.
